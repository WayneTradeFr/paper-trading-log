# Risk discipline log

Early learning log (June 2025 onward), paper trading only.
My later systematic work is in [quant-research](https://github.com/WayneTradeFr/quant-research).

## What went wrong
- Early demo accounts blown: no stop loss, revenge trading, oversized positions.
- March 2026: after a 9M loss, I put 90% of the account in a Bitcoin short
  with no stop. It worked, but it was a bet, not a trade: a 6% move against me
  would have wiped the account.

## Rules since then
1. Stop loss set before entry. No stop, no trade.
2. Maximum 1% risk per trade.
3. Every rule breach is written down.

## Why I publish this
Returns mean nothing without the risk taken to get them.
Moving to rule-based testing (backtests with in-sample / out-of-sample
validation) was the direct result of these mistakes.
